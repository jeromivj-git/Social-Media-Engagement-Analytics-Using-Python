# SOCIAL MEDIA ENGAGEMENT ANALYSIS 

## OBJECTIVE
This project analyzes social media engagement data to understand audience behavior, content performance, sentiment impact, device usage, and engagement trends using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Plotly.

---

# KEY FINDINGS

## CONTENT PERFORMANCE
- Video posts achieved the highest engagement rates, showing that audiences prefer dynamic and interactive content.
- Food was the best-performing content category, followed by Tech and Lifestyle.
- Brazil recorded the highest average engagement rate among all countries.
- Travel and Fashion categories showed comparatively lower engagement performance.

---

## USER TRENDS
- Users aged 46–55 showed the highest engagement rate.
- The 18–25 age group also demonstrated strong engagement activity.
- Verified and non-verified accounts showed minimal performance differences.
- Mature audiences contributed significantly to overall engagement.

---

## BEHAVIORAL INSIGHTS
- September recorded the highest average impressions.
- Mobile users had the highest average watch time compared to desktop and tablet users.
- Audience reach remained relatively stable throughout the year.
- Mobile-friendly content appears more effective for audience retention.

---

## SENTIMENT ANALYSIS
- Negative sentiment posts generated the highest engagement rate, likes, shares, and watch time.
- Neutral sentiment posts received the highest average comments.
- Positive sentiment posts showed comparatively lower engagement metrics.
- Emotionally intense and discussion-driven content attracted stronger audience interaction.

# CONCLUSION
The analysis shows that engagement is strongly influenced by content type, audience demographics, sentiment, and device usage. Video-based and emotionally engaging content performed best, while mobile users and mature audiences contributed significantly to platform interaction. These insights can help optimize future social media strategies and improve audience engagement.
